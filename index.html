Features

Everything you need to build real dressmaking skill

Six focused practice modules, built for beginners who want structure and for sewists who want sharper fundamentals.

📐

Pattern Drafting Drills

Timed exercises on bodice blocks, sleeve caps, and skirt panels. Draw the line, get instant feedback on proportion and symmetry before you cut fabric.

📏

Seam Allowance Calculator

Switch between 5/8", 3/8", and 1 cm allowances in seconds. It recalculates stitch lines, trim widths, and corner pivots so your pieces always match.

🧍

Fit-Adjustment Lessons

Guided walkthroughs for full-bust, swayback, and forward-shoulder adjustments. Each lesson shows the alteration, then asks you to apply it to a sample block.

🪡

Stitch-Tension Trainer

Simulated stitch plates let you adjust top and bobbin tension and see the resulting seam. Learn to spot loops, puckers, and skipped stitches without wasting thread.

📊

Progress Tracking

Every drill is scored and logged locally. Watch your drafting accuracy and tension accuracy trend upward across weeks of short daily sessions.

💾

Offline & Portable

No account, no cloud sync, no internet required. All lessons, measurements, and progress stay on your own machine in a single portable folder.
